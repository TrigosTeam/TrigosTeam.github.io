## About us

We study cancer evolution and cancer ecosystems through the analysis of multi-omic data and the development of computational methods.

Cancer cells resist treatment through many strategies, including reprogramming of the transcriptome, epigenome and other molecular layers, and remodelling of the surrounding microenvironment. Our lab investigates how these layers in tumour cells and the microenvironment adapt and co-evolve as an ecosystem to drive progression, resistance and metastasis.

Using a computational top-down approach on patient samples from biospecimen programmes and clinical trials, we aim to identify predictive biomarkers and therapeutic vulnerabilities that can be translated to the clinic.

## Research focus

* **Disease areas:** prostate cancer and multiple myeloma
* **Bulk and single-cell data:** genome sequencing, gene expression, methylation, chromatin accessibility, scRNA-seq and multiome
* **Spatial platforms:** Visium, OPAL, MIBI, CODEX and NanoString Digital Spatial Profiling
* **Imaging and AI:** multimodal outcome prediction integrating PET imaging with clinical and molecular data
* **Collaboration:** we work closely with medical oncologists, nuclear medicine physicians, radiation oncologists and surgeons
